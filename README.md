# The Tragedy of Doctor Faustus - class presentation

**Live presentation:** https://nidaocek.github.io/sunum/

A five-minute English presentation, prepared and presented by **Nida &Ouml;cek** for the B2 English preparatory programme at Istanbul Arel University (Module 1 book project).

## What this is

Each student in the class presented a book. This presentation follows **Project Option 1: "Introducing the Characters and Summarizing the Story"**:

- introduce the main and other characters, who they are and what they do;
- show where the story happens;
- tell the main events in order;
- no personal opinions, only the story.

The book is a simplified (graded reader) edition of *Doctor Faustus* by Christopher Marlowe. A scholar from Wittenberg gives his soul to Lucifer in return for twenty-four years of power and the devil Mephistophilis as his servant. When the twenty-four years end, the devils come for him at midnight.

## How to use it

Open the live link in a modern browser (tested in Microsoft Edge) and press F11 for full screen.

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| Right arrow, Space, Page Down | Next slide |
| Left arrow, Page Up | Previous slide |
| Home / End | First / last slide |
| N | Show or hide the speaker notes |

On a phone or tablet, swipe left or right.

Some slides are interactive:
- **Slide 2:** move the mouse over a character card to bring its picture forward.
- **Slide 6:** move the mouse over a sin to light its ring of fire; the figures in the background follow the mouse.
- **Slide 7:** click an event heading to show its picture behind the text.
- **Slides 3, 4, 5, 8 and 9:** the scenes play on their own.

## How it is made

- One self-contained file, `index.html`, with all the CSS, JavaScript and inline SVG drawings. No frameworks, no external fonts, no downloads.
- `img/characters/` holds the first versions of the character drawings. The current deck draws the slide 2 pictures inside `index.html` and no longer loads these files.
- All illustrations are original drawings made for this presentation, in a flat storybook style. They are not copied from the book.
- The animations run only on the slide on screen. With the system setting "reduce motion" turned on, they show still pictures instead.
- The background fire grows slowly through the story and is strongest in "The last hour".
- Built with the help of Claude Code (an AI coding assistant); the commits list Claude as co-author.
